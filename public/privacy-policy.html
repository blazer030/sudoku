Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Sudoku mobile application ("the App", "we") handles your information. We aim to collect as little data as possible and never sell it.

Information stored on your device

The App stores the following data only on your device, using your device's local storage:

  • Your in-progress game (board state, timer, undo history)
  • Your settings (color theme, gameplay preferences, launcher icon preference)
  • Your game statistics (completion times, hint usage, recent results)

This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the App permanently removes it.

Analytics

On Android and on the web version, the App uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand aggregate usage and improve the game. The information collected is not tied to your name, email address, or Google account. It includes:

  • Device model, operating system version, and app version
  • Device identifiers: a random app-instance ID generated by Firebase, and your device's Android Advertising ID. On the web, Google Analytics stores a random client ID in a first-party cookie
  • Approximate location (country or region), derived by Google from your IP address
  • App activity: screens viewed, session length, and game events — when a game starts, completes, or is abandoned (with its difficulty, time, and hints used), when a hint is used, walkthrough use, and per-game undo and mistake counts

This data is used only for analytics. It is not used for advertising, and the App shows no ads. We do not collect your name, email, precise location, contacts, photos, or any other personally identifiable information. Google Firebase's own privacy practices apply to the data they receive: see Firebase Privacy Information.

Permissions

The App requests no runtime permissions. Its install-time permissions are used for:

  • Internet and network state — to send analytics events
  • Google Play Billing — reserved for optional in-app purchases; none are offered at this time
  • Advertising ID, ad-services attribution, install referrer, and wake lock — used by Firebase Analytics for usage measurement and reliable event delivery

Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so we can investigate.

Your choices

  • You can reset or delete your Android Advertising ID at any time in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on your device).
  • On the web, clearing the site's cookies resets the client ID, and browser tracking protection can block collection.
  • Uninstalling the App stops all analytics collection and permanently removes all locally stored data.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top. Substantive changes will be noted in the App's release notes.
