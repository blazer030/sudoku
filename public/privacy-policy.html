Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Sudoku mobile application ("the App", "we") handles your information. We aim to collect as little data as possible and never sell it.

Information stored on your device

The App stores the following data only on your device, using your device's local storage:

  • Your in-progress game (board state, timer, undo history)
  • Your settings (color theme, gameplay preferences, launcher icon preference)
  • Your game statistics (completion times, hint usage, recent results)

This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the App permanently removes it.

Anonymous analytics

On Android, the App uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand aggregate usage and improve the game. The information collected is anonymous — it is not tied to a personal identity. It includes:

  • Device model, operating system version, and app version
  • Approximate country (derived from IP address, then discarded)
  • Anonymous event signals: when a game starts, completes, or is abandoned; when a hint is used; when the donate page is opened or a tip is purchased

We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos, or any other personally identifiable information. Google Firebase's own privacy practices apply to the data they receive: see Firebase Privacy Information.

In-app purchases

The App offers an optional Donate tip jar via Google Play Billing. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play — the App never sees your payment method or billing details. We only receive a confirmation that a purchase succeeded and which tier was selected. Refer to Google's Privacy Policy for how Google processes payment information.

Permissions

The App requests only the Internet permission, used to send anonymous analytics events and process Google Play Billing.

Children's privacy

The App is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so we can investigate.

Your choices

  • You can opt out of Firebase Analytics by disabling personalized advertising and usage reporting in your device's Google account settings.
  • You can clear all locally stored data by uninstalling the App.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top. Substantive changes will be noted in the App's release notes.
